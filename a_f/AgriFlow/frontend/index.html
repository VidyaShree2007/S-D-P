How AgriFlow works

Three kinds of accounts, one supply chain.

Farmers

List fresh crops with quantity and price, then confirm and deliver the orders that come in.

🏪

🛒

Buyers

Search the marketplace, compare farmers and vendors, and click Buy Now.

Track stock, orders and revenue at a glance, with charts of your best crops.
